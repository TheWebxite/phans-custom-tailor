3Plain-language explanations

Use this picture when explaining to Thinh:

TermWhat it means
DomainThe website's address, like a street address, e.g. phanscustomtailor.com. Paid yearly, owned by the business.
HostingThe “house” the website lives in: the server that stores content and photos so the site is open 24/7. The domain is the address; hosting is the house at that address.
Pointing the domain (DNS)Changing the directions so the address leads to the new house. We do it; we only need access to the domain account.
https (padlock)The site is secure. Included, free.
RedirectsOld Google links lead to the matching new pages, so Phan's keeps its search ranking.
HaravanThe platform behind the old site (software and hosting in one). Keep it until the new site is live, then stop it.
KoalendarThe booking tool Phan's already uses. The new site just links to it.
Payment gatewayA service for paying online by card or wallet. In Vietnam usually OnePay or VNPay. Only needed for online sales (phase 2).
MaintenanceAfter launch: monitoring, updates, backups, fixes and small content changes.

4Our position (agreed with Xin)

  1. Phase 1 scope: three-language site (English main, Vietnamese, Chinese); prices; process; fabrics; booking; WhatsApp and Zalo; showcase shop; old articles moved over; Google optimisation.
  2. Domain: Phan's already owns phanscustomtailor.com (on Google Maps) and tailormadesuit.net. Suggest phanscustomtailor.com as main since it matches the brand; tailormadesuit.net forwards to it. No new domain needed. If he prefers tailormadesuit.net as main, that's fine too. The domain always stays in Phan's name.
  3. Hosting: TWX handles everything: buying and managing hosting, security, backups, maintenance. Hosting and maintenance costs will be in the quote.
  4. Booking: keep his Koalendar calendar; the site's booking buttons link to it. No new system; he manages his calendar as today.
  5. Shop: phase 1 is showcase only. Customers browse cloth and ready-made pieces, then ask about price on WhatsApp/Zalo or book a visit. No cart, no login, no online payment.
  6. Why not sell online yet: it needs someone handling orders daily, packing, international shipping, returns, and a payment gateway (OnePay, VNPay). For made-to-measure, customers still come to the villa to be measured, so a showcase is enough. Online sales can be phase 2.
  7. Customer accounts and cart on the old site: not needed in phase 1 (the old shop has one product).
  8. Timeline: about 1 week after we receive everything in section 7.
  9. Web 3D team: complementary, not competing. The website is the main home; their 3D experience can go on a page or section inside it. We're happy to coordinate.
