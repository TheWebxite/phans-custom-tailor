Does the shop need online payment?

Not at first. Customers browse Nobility cloth and ready-made pieces, then message or book a visit. For made-to-measure, they still come in to be measured.

Selling online needs someone on orders every day, packing, overseas shipping, returns and a payment gateway such as OnePay or VNPay. When you're ready, that's a later phase.
