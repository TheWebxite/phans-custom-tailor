C. PLAIN-LANGUAGE EXPLANATIONS
• Domain: the website's address, like a street address (e.g. phanscustomtailor.com). Paid yearly, owned by the business.
• Hosting: the "house" the website lives in: the server that stores content and photos so the site is open 24/7. The domain is the address; hosting is the house at that address.
• Pointing the domain (DNS): when moving house, we change the directions so the old address leads to the new house. We do this; we only need access to the domain account.
• https / the padlock in the browser: the site is secure. Included, free.
• Redirects: old Google links automatically lead to the matching new pages, so Phan's keeps its search ranking.
• Haravan: the platform behind the current site (software and hosting in one). Keep it until the new site is live, then stop it.
• Vercel: where the demo runs; a global server network, so the site loads fast in any country.
• Koalendar: the booking tool Phan's already uses. The new site just links to it.
• Payment gateway: a service that lets customers pay online by card or wallet. In Vietnam usually OnePay or VNPay. (Razorpay from Malaysia doesn't serve Vietnamese businesses.)
• Maintenance: after launch we monitor, update, back up, fix issues and make small content changes.

D. OUR POSITION (AGREED WITH YONG)
1) Phase 1 scope: three-language site (English main, Vietnamese, Chinese); prices; process; fabrics; booking; WhatsApp and Zalo; showcase shop; old articles moved over; Google optimisation.
2) Domain: Phan's already owns phanscustomtailor.com (on Google Maps) and tailormadesuit.net. We suggest phanscustomtailor.com as the main address since it matches the brand; tailormadesuit.net forwards to it. No new domain needed. If he prefers tailormadesuit.net as main, we do it the other way round. The domain always stays in Phan's name.
3) Hosting: TWX handles it all: buying and managing hosting, security, backups, maintenance. Hosting and maintenance costs will be in the quote.
4) Booking: keep the Koalendar calendar he already uses; the site's booking buttons link to it. No new booking system; he manages his calendar as today.
5) Shop: phase 1 is showcase only. Customers browse cloth and ready-made pieces, then ask about price on WhatsApp/Zalo or book a visit. No cart, no login, no online payment.
   Why: selling online needs someone handling orders daily, packing, international shipping, returns, and signing up for a payment gateway (OnePay, VNPay). For made-to-measure, customers still come to the villa to be measured, so a showcase is enough. If he wants online sales later, that's phase 2.
6) Customer accounts and cart on the old site: not needed in phase 1 (the old shop has only one product).
7) Timeline: about 1 week after we receive everything in section G.
8) Web 3D team: complementary, not competing. The website is the main home; their 3D experience can go on a page or section inside it. We're happy to coordinate with them.
9) Using AI: be upfront. TWX is a software and AI company; AI makes us faster and better, but design, testing and support are done by real people.
