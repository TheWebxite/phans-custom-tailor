Context

Most of Phan's customers are from overseas, and they decide before they book their flight

Phan's already has an excellent reputation with international customers. A visitor is usually in Saigon for only a few days, so the website has to answer their questions up front:

  • How much a suit, shirt or trousers cost
  • How long it takes and how many visits
  • Which fabrics are available
  • How to book while still abroad
  • Can they trust it: real reviews from past customers

Current website

Where overseas visitors get stuck today

  • Choosing Chinese still shows English, mixed with Vietnamese system text such as “Chưa có sản phẩm nào…” and “Mua ngay”.
  • Prices and turnaround are only in the FAQ.
  • The Suit, Pants and Dress Shirt tabs are empty; the shop has one product.
  • The Testimonials section is empty, despite 2,000+ great reviews online.
  • Blog posts mix three languages on one page.
CauseThe Haravan shop template is built for retail, not for made-to-measure by appointment.

Highlight: AI fitting room

Customers try clothes on with their own camera, right on the website

  • Tap “Try-On” on any product: the AI dresses the visitor in it through their phone or laptop camera within seconds.
  • Moves with the body in real time: turn, raise an arm, button up or bend over, and the cloth and its folds follow.
  • Quick comparisons: switch to another piece in one tap, then save or share the result.
WhyOverseas visitors see themselves in Phan's clothes before they arrive, so they book with more confidence. Real measurements are still taken at the villa; the AI helps them choose style, colour and cloth.

Why three languages

Every customer reads the whole site in their own language

EnglishMain language: most customers are from overseas.
中文Visitors from China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia.
Tiếng ViệtLocal customers and overseas Vietnamese.
  • Fully translated, never mixed: menus, buttons, prices, FAQ, forms, articles.
  • Separate web address per language (/vi/, /zh/), so Google shows the right version for searches like “custom suit Saigon” or “胡志明市 定制西装”.

Menu

A full-screen menu that is clear and easy to tap on a phone

  • Nine numbered items that follow the questions customers ask: process, prices, fabrics, shop, contact…
  • Hovering an item highlights it, shows the “P” monogram and a preview photo.
  • At the bottom: social links, language switch and booking.
WhyThe old menu had items like “Men Measureguide” and “Search”, so visitors didn't know where to start.
